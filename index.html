Mert Cemri

 

Mert Cemri Ph.D. Student at UC Berkeley Berkeley, CA, United States

Email: cemri@berkeley.edu

[Google Scholar] [Curriculum Vitae]

I am a Ph.D. student in the Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) and Sky Computing Labs at UC Berkeley, advised by Ion Stoica, Kannan Ramchandran, and Alex Dimakis.

I am also a part of the Berkeley Laboratory for Information and System Sciences (BLISS) and serve as an organizer of the BLISS Seminar. Feel free to contact me if you want to participate in our seminar!

My research focuses on efficient machine learning and agentic systems, including multi-agent large language model (LLM) systems, test-time scaling, and discrete generative models.

Experience

I am currently a student researcher in the Systems Research Group at Google, working on AI for systems: using LLM-driven agents to accelerate systems performance research and to synthesize formally verified systems components. I previously interned as a machine learning engineer at Apple, working on agentic post-training for foundation models, and conducted research at EPFL with Ali H. Sayed on distributed optimization and social learning, and at Bilkent University with Tolga Çukur and Aykut Koç on neural methods for natural language and graph-structured data.
