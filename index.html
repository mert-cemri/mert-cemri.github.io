About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) and Sky Computing Labs at UC Berkeley, advised by Ion Stoica, Kannan Ramchandran, and Alex Dimakis.

My research focuses on efficient machine learning and agentic systems, including multi-agent large language model (LLM) systems, LLM-driven discovery and optimization, test-time scaling, and discrete generative models.

I am also a part of the Berkeley Laboratory for Information and System Sciences (BLISS) and an organizer of the BLISS Seminar. Feel free to reach out if you would like to give a talk.
